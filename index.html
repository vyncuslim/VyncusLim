Who I Am

I am a student developer based in Malaysia. Currently a Year 7 student, I push the boundaries of what is possible at the intersection of AI, health technology, cybersecurity, and Earth observation. I design privacy-first sleep analytics platforms, focusing on how personal data can help users reflect on their wellness.

My work centers on turning health telemetry and sleep patterns into actionable insights, advocating for a "Sleep OS" philosophy that works seamlessly without clinical intervention or wearable constraints. I also build advanced web security mechanisms, such as AI-native behavioral verification, to ensure user privacy and verify authentic human presence.
